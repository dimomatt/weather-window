      A planning filter, not a forecast. Read the full avalanche forecast and check road and trail status before you go.

      Ski ratings combine each zone's headline (highest) avalanche danger with the line's terrain class (ATES) in a conservative matrix. Catalog distances, gains and elevations are approximate; ski-line aspects and elevations follow Front Range Ski Mountaineering and Powder Project route pages. Modeled snow depth comes from a weather model grid and can miss local wind-scour or drifts.
